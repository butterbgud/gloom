## Event cards

| # | Card title | Ability / effect |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Body Thief | Remove one living character and one dead character from play. |
| 2 | A Tragic Misunderstanding | Swap the top Modifiers on two living characters. |
| 3 | To Be or Not To Be | Move one Untimely Death from a dead character to a living character with negative Self-Worth. |
| 4 | A Stormy Night | Draw four cards, play one, then discard down to the draw limit. |
| 5 | The Root of All Evil | Draw one card from each opponent's hand and immediately play those you wish; keep the rest. |
| 6 | A Second Chance | Cancel an Untimely Death as it is played, or remove one from a character. |
| 7 | Misfortune Favors the Old | Play two additional negative cards this round. |
| 8 | Til Death Do Us Part | Play an Untimely Death on any character with the heart icon, regardless of Self-Worth. |
| 9 | Smoke and Mirrors | Cancel an Event as it is played. |
| 10 | Twist of Fate | Replace the top Modifier on a character with one from your hand; keep the replaced card. |
| 11 | A Chance to Begin Again | Discard all Modifiers from one living character. |
| 12 | An Unpleasant Surprise | Remove the top Modifier from one living character and discard it. |
